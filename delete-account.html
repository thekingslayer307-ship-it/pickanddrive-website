Delete your account

You can delete your Pick&Drive account at any time, and you do not need to ask us to do it for you.

From the app

  1. Open Pick&Drive and go to Profile (riders) or Account (captains).
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. You are signed out immediately and your details are erased.

If you are on a trip, finish or cancel it first — we will not delete an account with a journey still in progress.

If you no longer have the app

Email us from the address or with the mobile number on the account and we will delete it for you within 30 days.

support@pickanddrive.pk

What is deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Name, mobile number, emailErased. The number and email are released so they can be used again.
Profile photoDeleted from our storage.
Captain documents (CNIC, licence, vehicle)Deleted.
Saved places and payment preferenceDeleted.
Messages with your captain or riderDetached from you.
Completed trips and paymentsKept, without your details attached.
Why completed trips are kept. A finished journey has a fare, a commission and a counterparty. We are required to keep those financial records, and the other person on each trip keeps their own history and any right to raise a complaint about it. Those records no longer identify you.

How long it takes

Deleting from the app is immediate. Requests by email are completed within 30 days. Backups are overwritten on their normal cycle, within 90 days.
