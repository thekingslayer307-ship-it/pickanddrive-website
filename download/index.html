Installing

  1. Tap Download the app above and wait for it to finish.
  2. Open the downloaded file from your notifications or the Files app.
  3. Android will ask whether to allow installs from this source — allow it for your browser, then go back and tap Install.
  4. If Play Protect warns you about an unknown app, choose Install anyway. It shows this for any app not yet distributed through the Play Store.

Signing in
