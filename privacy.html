Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what information Pick&Drive collects through its app and website, why, and how it's used. It applies to riders, drivers, and anyone using the Pick&Drive customer app, driver app, or admin console.

Information we collect

  • Account details — your name and mobile number when you sign up, and your email if you sign in with Google.
  • Location — your pickup and drop-off points when you book a ride, and a driver's live location while a trip is in progress, so it can be shown to you and to our dispatch team.
  • Driver documents — for driver accounts, we collect CNIC, driving licence, vehicle registration, and a selfie photo for identity verification before a driver is approved to accept rides.
  • Ride and payment records — pickup/drop addresses, fare, category, payment method, and status for every ride you take or drive.
  • Messages — in-trip chat messages between a rider and driver, and any support/complaint messages sent to our team.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account (we use a one-time code sent to your phone instead of a password).
  • To calculate fares, dispatch a driver, and show live trip status and location.
  • To review and approve driver applications and documents.
  • To respond to support requests and complaints.
  • To calculate driver earnings and process wallet withdrawals.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. Location search and map display are handled through OpenStreetMap and its Nominatim geocoding service. If you choose to sign in with Google, Google processes your sign-in per its own privacy policy. We may share ride and account details with law enforcement if legally required to do so.

Your choices

You can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account by contacting us using the details below. Location access can be turned off at any time in your device settings, though this will limit the app's ability to find your pickup point or show live tracking.

Data retention

We keep ride, account, and document records for as long as your account is active, and afterward for as long as reasonably needed for legal, safety, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Not a monitored emergency service

The in-app SOS button dials a configured emergency contact number directly — it is a shortcut, not a live-monitored safety system. For a genuine emergency, contact local emergency services directly.

Children

Pick&Drive is not directed at children and account holders must be old enough to hold a valid CNIC or otherwise legally able to use ride-hailing services in Pakistan.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date at the top of the page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to inexiapk@gmail.com.
